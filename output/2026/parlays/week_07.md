# Week 7 long-shot parlays

Every parlay is at least +1000 at fair odds and no player appears in more than one parlay; a parlay stops once it reaches +1000, and if the strong legs run out it is left out rather than padded. Legs are alternate milestones ("X+") the model gives about 70%+ after calibration, cleared in at least 3 of the player's last 5 games, with a projection not far above what he has actually done; TD legs blend the simulation with the stats-driven TD model (volume, red-zone and end-zone usage, scoring environment), on stat types the model has graded well this season, one leg per game so they're close to independent. Probabilities assume independence; books add extra margin to parlays, so compare the payout with the fair odds before betting.
