Leans the model's side on every other posted line: information, not bets

Win % is the model's probability for the lean side after correcting for its measured overconfidence (backtests showed the raw simulation is too sure of itself). Edge is the expected return per unit at the assumed price; check the real price before betting.
Season log: one square per game this season, filled when the lean side would have won against today's line.
